Srigiri, Ongole

A living kshetram of the Vaikhanasa Agama tradition — its history, governance, and daily life.

How Srigiri Began

Founding

Sri Siddeswari Peeta Palitha Srigiri Sri Venkateswara Swamy Vari Devasthanam was constructed by Sri Aluru Venkata Ramana Rao, his family, relatives, and close associates. The temple opened on 16th June 1997 and was handed over to Sri Sri Sri Siva Chidananda Bharathi Swamiji, then Peethadhipathi of Sri Siddheswari Peetam, Courtallam, Tamil Nadu.

Kshetra Palaka

Sri Abhaya Anjaneya Swamy temple was built by Sri Aluru Jwala Ramarao and Smt Suseela Devi, with sons Phani Kumar and Saravana Kumar (USA).

Present Leadership

The temple is run by His Holiness Sri Sri Sri Siddheswarananda Bharathi Swamiji, Courtallam. Detailed accounts are sent to the Peetam periodically.

Detailed Operations

Governance, Deities & Daily Life

Governance — The Dharmakarthas

Five hereditary trustees, each responsible for a distinct area of the temple's operations, unchanged since founding.
1. Sri Aluru Venkata Ramana Rao Founder Dharmakartha (Vyavastapaka), renowned Astrologer
2. Sri Aluru Jwala Rama Rao Retd. Divisional Accounts Officer (W), AP Govt. — in charge of Arjitha Sevas and Prasadams. Built Srivari potu, storeroom, dharmakarthas office, guest house.
3. Sri Aluru Kumar Athreya S/o late Sri Aluru Ramakrishna Rao, Retd. Joint Director, Finance Dept., AP — in charge of Srivari Strong Room, gold, silver, and clothes. Built the strong room with Godrej locking to bank specification; also oversees Ghovu Kshetram.
4. Sri Chilamakuri Venkata Rama Krishna Rao Retd. Sub-Divisional Engineer, BSNL — in charge of Construction, Infrastructure, Parakamani Uthsavam, Hundi, and Srigiri Dharma Prachara Parishad. Built the Archakas' guest house, Srinivasa Vanam.
5. Sri Aluru Venkateswara Rao Dharmakartha-5 — jointly manages the Gosamrakshana Samithi's financial transactions.

With the founder Dharmakartha's support, the Srivari Vimanam and Mahamandapam took their present shape.

Sacred Deities & Offerings

The Mula Varlu is carved from Krishna Sila — said to resound with the syllable Om — at a scale nearly equal to Tirumala's deity.

Sri Vari Mula Varlu: offered by Sri Moravaneni Kotaiah, Smt Venkata Subbamma, and their sons, of Yerrabalem village, Korsepadu Mandal. The 8'8" Mula Varlu was carved from Krishna Sila by Maha Silpi Sri N. Ratnachari of Chennai.

Sri Vari Utsava Murthulu: (Srivaru with consorts Sridevi and Bhoodevi) was offered by Sri Devathu Musala Rao and Smt Kusuma Kumari of Ongole.

Gold & silver ornaments: 108 gold flowers were offered by the founder in 2006, used monthly for the Srivari Suvarna Ashtadala Padma Paadarchana Seva. The gold crown (Suvarna Kireetam) was donated by Sri Aluru Ramakoteswara Vamsee (USA). The Bangaru Chatari was donated by sixteen close associates of the Dharmakarthas' families.

Temple Staff & Honorary Roles

The temple follows Vaikhanasa Aagamana Sastra. Three Archakas and one Veda Parayandar serve full-time, alongside honorary posts unique to Srigiri.

The Archaka selection committee — Sri Dr. Ganjam Srinivasa Murthy, Sri Parankusam Seetharama Charyulu, and Sri Maddipati Anantha Sesha Charyulu — oversees selection, with final approval from Swamiji. All staff are paid monthly, by bank transfer, for daily-wage service.

Sannidhi Golla Sri Eerla Narasimha Rao Yadav's family — opens the Sanctum Sanctorum first, ahead of the Archakas' sevas.
Legal Officer Shri NVS Talpa Sayee — retired District Judge, formerly TTD Law Officer.
Chief Security Advisor Shri G. Surya Prakasa Rao, IPS — retired Inspector General of Police, Coastal Security.
Aasthana Gaurava Naada Swara Vidwan Sri Pathakamuru Sambaiah.
Temple Barber (Nayi Brahmin) Honorary post for tonsure and neelala samarpana.

Transparency & Finance

No government or TTD funding. No direct cash donations. Every offering is Hundi-routed and independently counted.

Initially the temple had no Hundi. After repeated devotee requests, Sri Sri Sri Siva Chidananda Bharathi Swamiji directed one be provided; it was ceremonially opened by Sri G. Surya Prakasa Rao, IPS, then Addl. Superintendent of Police, Prakasam Dist. Hundi operations are banked with Union Bank of India.

Parakamani Utsavam: the periodic Hundi-counting event is conducted by a team of 40–60 — Union Bank employees, Dharmakarthas, and devotees drawn at random, including government officials, advocates, doctors, and businesspeople. The counted amount is announced to Lord Balaji by Dharmakartha-4, then credited to the temple account by Union Bank officials on the spot.

Visesha Parakamani: ornament appraisal is conducted periodically by a bank appraiser and insurance officer, in the presence of two district-cadre judicial officers. Item-wise weight and quality are recorded and the ornaments insured with a general insurance company.

Community Service

The Srigiri Gosamrakshana Samithi and Sri Giri Vidya Nidhi extend the temple's mission beyond its walls — to cows and to students.

Srigiri Gosamrakshana Samithi (Goshala): was promoted and funded by Sri K.B.M.V. Subramanyam and Sri Jada Koteswara Rao Yadav. Milk and dairy products from the goshala are used for daily temple rituals; the cows are insured and monitored by veterinary doctors, with finances managed by Dharmakarthas 3 and 5 through Andhra Bank, Ongole.

Sri Giri Vidya Nidhi: provides financial assistance to economically backward Hindu students who have earned academic distinction. Its principal patrons are Founder Chairman Sri A.V. Ramana Rao and his sons, Dr. Srinivas Charan, Rajeev (USA), and Jai Sankar, together with the Dharmakarthas.

Prasadam schemes: Sasvatha Archana, Sasvatha Abhishekam, and Sasvatha Uchitha Prasadam — each scheme's funds are deposited into a dedicated bank account.

Srigiri Chinmaya Balavihar

Sunday spiritual classes for children aged 5–17, run in partnership with Chinmaya Mission.

Srigiri Devasthanam has partnered with Chinmaya Mission to conduct Sunday spiritual classes — Balavihar — for children aged 5 to 17.

Chinmaya Mission Swamijis from the Prakasam, Nellore, and Guntur districts support this program, helping impart cultural values, sacred scriptures, and spiritual discipline to the next generation.

📵 Mobile Phones Strictly Prohibited

Mobile phones are not permitted inside the temple premises. Management reserves the right to confiscate any phone found inside. Devotees are kindly requested to comply with this policy, in keeping with the sanctity and spiritual power of this sacred space.

Visitor Etiquette & Guidelines

  • Footwear is removed before entering the temple premises.
  • Traditional dress is customary.
  • Arjitha Sevas require traditional wear — panche or dhoti for men, saree for women.
  • Photography is strictly restricted inside Srigiri Temple.
